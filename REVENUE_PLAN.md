## Success Factors

1. **Speed beats perfection** - Launch on PH with a simple video, don't wait for polish
2. **Discounts drive urgency** - Always have a time-limited offer running
3. **Follow up relentlessly** - 80% of sales come after the 3rd contact
4. **Show, don't tell** - Demo videos convert better than screenshots
5. **Social proof everything** - Even 3 testimonials boost conversion 30%+
